Document 03 · Behavioural specification

What the bot actually feels like

A golden-path conversation followed by the edge cases that define the safety, grounding and independence requirements.

Profile under testAarav, 18, Lucknow, 84% in Class 12, 78.4 percentile, family income around ₹6 lakh/year. He believes IIT/NIT is his only route and is considering an expensive new-age college.

Scenario 1 · The golden path

Turn 01 · StabiliseStage 1 / Rail 1
Aarav
78 percentile aaya. IIT/NIT to possible nahi hai. Life khatam lag rahi hai.
Bot
78 percentile after a full year of prep genuinely stings—main pretend nahi karunga ki it doesn't. But you're not the exception: most JEE candidates do not get a JoSAA seat, and there are still real routes. Pehle ek quick check: are you safe right now, or are you thinking of hurting yourself?

Specific validation, useful context and a safety check happen before advice.

Turns 02–03 · LocateStage 2 / batched intake
Aarav
Safe hu. Lucknow, boards 84%. Papa around 6 lakh kamaate hain. Budget ka idea nahi, loan lena padega.
Bot
Samajh gaya. Main tumhare liye rank, family affordability, options outside IIT/NIT, and what to verify before paying map karunga. Is loan ko comfortable EMI ke hisaab se dekhna hai ya maximum possible budget ke hisaab se?

Income is a decision input, not a lead score. Questions are batched to reduce cost and cognitive load.

Turn 04 · WidenStage 3 / Decision D2
Bot
Tumhare map mein six branches hain: JoSAA/CSAB spot rounds, home-state options, established private exams, new-age colleges, another attempt, and BCA/BSc CS plus self-learning. Main sirf apni eight colleges ko “best” nahi bolunga—pehle map dekhna zaroori hai.

The bot shows non-commercial options and discloses that it does not have verified data for every branch.

Turns 05–07 · Stress-testStage 4 / mandatory gate
Bot
Roughly, banks may assess borrowing around 4× annual family income, so ₹6L income suggests a ceiling near ₹24L—not a promise. Loans above ₹7.5L often need collateral. If a ₹18L course is financed over seven years, the EMI can be around ₹28–30k/month depending on rate. If placement takes six months, who carries that EMI? Also: do you want GATE, government work or an MS later? The degree-granting university matters for those routes.

All numbers come from deterministic compute or verified facts. College names remain gated until loan, downside and degree checks complete.

Turn 08 · NarrowStage 5 / caution invariant
Bot
Based on the budget and risk you described, I would not put Newton at ₹26.9L first. NIAT is more financially plausible to investigate, but verify the reported average placement number and ask whether it is for the full batch. A cheaper BCA/BSc route with a strong portfolio may be safer if the family cannot absorb the downside.

A recommendation always has a caution. Advising against an expensive option is an intended outcome.

Turn 09 · EquipStage 6 / audit pack
Bot
Before paying any deposit, ask: (1) Which university is on the final degree certificate? (2) How many students got paid internships and what was the median stipend? (3) Is the quoted fee all-in? (4) What is the refund policy after semester one? (5) Can you speak to a current student they did not select for you?

The student receives something useful even if they never convert.

Turns 10–11 · Hand offStage 7 / Decision D5
Bot
If you choose NIAT, I can connect you to an independent NIAT senior for ₹99/30 minutes; they are paid by us, not NIAT. If you prefer, talk to your dad first. I can check in in about three weeks—reply STOP anytime.

The handoff is college-specific, offered once, and followed by consent-based follow-up.

What would make this conversation wrong?

  • Showing a college list before the financial gate.
  • Giving a number without a source, date or deterministic calculation.
  • Using “don’t worry” instead of specific emotional acknowledgement.
  • Giving a recommendation without an honest caution.
  • Claiming verified data for an unlisted institution.
  • Offering a generic mentor link instead of the college-specific handoff.
