Project Abstract & Thesis

Every year in India, roughly 14.5 lakh students take the JEE Main exam competing for just 67,323 JoSAA seats across IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. The remaining ~95% (~13 lakh candidates) must navigate an opaque private education market surrounded by admission consultants who earn ₹20,000–₹60,000 per referral.

Campus Critique WhatsApp Bot is an autonomous conversational decision-support infrastructure engineered with zero admission kickbacks. It enforces an empathetic 7-stage state machine that calculates exact all-India ranks, runs loan affordability stress-tests ($4\times$ household income limits), delivers institutional recommendations with mandatory cautionary caveats, and provides candidates with an admissions audit checklist.
