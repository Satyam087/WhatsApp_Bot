01

The Macro Admission Funnel

Every June, approximately thirteen to fifteen lakh Indian engineering aspirants face a high-consequence financial commitment within an intense four-to-six-week counseling window, advised almost exclusively by agents with an explicit commercial interest in their enrollment.

~14.5L JEE Main Registrations
67,323 Total JoSAA Public Seats
~95% Excluded from JoSAA (~13L)
20–30% Dropper Rank Improvement

The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) allocates seats across 23 IITs, 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and 56 GFTIs. The structural gap between candidate volume and subsidized public capacity produces an addressable cohort of over 1.3 million unplaced candidates annually. For this demographic, private technical education represents an opaque and predatory marketplace.

04

Product Vision & Locked Decision D1

Locked Decision D1 · Structural Operational Disinterest

The bot’s primary performance metric is never admissions. The platform accepts zero commercial referral revenue from any college. The system must possess the structural capability and conversational duty to state:

“Based on your family’s annual income and risk parameters, you should not spend ₹18–24 lakh on any institution in this category. Here is what I would do instead.”

The operational metric advised_against_spending_rate must remain strictly greater than zero (target: 10%–20%). A 0% rate over any 30-day window is classified as a Severity-0 bug.

The 7-Stage Conversational Model

Rather than deploying a rigid decision tree or an unconstrained chatbot, the system executes an empathetic 7-stage state machine modeled after an authoritative, grounded college senior.

MoSCoW Functional Requirements Matrix

08

Messaging UI/UX & WhatsApp Rules

Linguistic Register & Persona Guidelines

The bot converses in an authentic, respectful Hinglish senior register (an experienced college senior or older sibling who has successfully navigated counseling). Explicit rules:

  • Banned Superficial Cheer: Never use "Don't worry, everything happens for a reason!" Instead use: "78.4 percentile after an entire year of preparation genuinely stings. I am not going to sugarcoat that."
  • Banned Promotional Vocabulary: Automated regex filters block: "guaranteed placement", "100% placement", "limited seats", "hurry up", "instant admission".
  • Message Length Constraints: Every outbound bubble is constrained to under 140 words to prevent cognitive fatigue.

Transcript-Derived KPI Framework (Decision D4)

100% of performance metrics are derived programmatically from the conversational transcript without intrusive surveys.

advised_against_spending_rate ★ Conversations where Stage 4 resulted in negative spending recommendation 10%–20% (>0%) Proof of operational independence. 0% for a month is a P0 bug.
stage_depth_median Deepest stage reached across all sessions (1 to 7) ≥ 5.0 Validates conversational engagement and low drop-off.
stress_test_completion_rate Conversations completing all three tests (Loan, Downside, Degree) ≥ 60% Measures adherence to the financial gate.

ungrounded_numeric_claims Numbers in output missing from retrieved Fact Block 0 (Zero) Absolute protection against fabricated fees or salaries.
distress_routing_recall Academic crisis triggers routed to Tele-MANAS 100% (Zero Error) Safety invariant for vulnerable adolescents.

Ethics, Safety & Tele-MANAS (14416)

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance for Minors

  • Isolated Data Silo (Decision D9): Minors' phone numbers, family income, and session data live in a dedicated Supabase schema (wa) completely separated from web application databases.
  • No National Identifiers: The system strictly prohibits collecting Aadhaar numbers, application passwords, or banking credentials.
  • Session Purge Policies: Session transcripts are automatically anonymized following the close of each admission cycle.

Mental Health Crisis Protocol: Tele-MANAS (14416)

The system integrates Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1800-891-4416), the national 24/7 mental health counseling helpline launched by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) with NIMHANS as the nodal agency, spanning 53 tele-mental health operational cells across 36 states and UTs. When acute distress or self-harm ideation is detected, the counseling funnel halts immediately, a supportive response is delivered, and emergency alerts are dispatched to platform administrators.

Product invariants

  1. Crisis signals stop the funnel immediately.
  2. College recommendations require loan, downside and degree checks.
  3. Every number is computed, looked up from a versioned fact, or omitted.
  4. Every recommendation includes a caution; “none are safe” is valid.
  5. Students can reply STOP to end follow-up.
